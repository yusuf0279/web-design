# Design inspiration field notes

**Visited:** 2026-10-04  
**Purpose:** build a cross-sector source list for future website and product design work. These notes describe gallery organization and examples surfaced during browsing; an item appearing in a gallery is not proof that its design or interaction is effective.

## Direct observations

### Godly / Recent Design

- Opening `godly.website` landed on Recent. The rendered index groups Websites, OG Images, App Screenshots, App Icons, Tools, Skills, and Jobs. Cards combine creator/source links with style/color tags and engagement details; an example page gives its poster image more room than the metadata.
- Opened **Hairline Isometric Icons** (light geometric monochrome illustration), **Mobile Wallet** (pale landing page with NFC card mockup and scroll animation), and **IT WILL FIND YOU** (dark, vibrant abstract 3D/Y2K motion).
- Useful: separate browsing by medium, style, color, and interaction. Limit: some cards point to a source post rather than a complete live website, so the site's section sequence may be missing.
- Sources: [Recent, reached through Godly](https://recent.design/?ref=godly), [Hairline Isometric Icons](https://recent.design/i/6k2s8wq-hairline-isometric-icon-library), [Mobile Wallet](https://recent.design/i/p7jwe4i-mobile-wallet-credentials-landing), [IT WILL FIND YOU](https://recent.design/i/mo1hnx2-infinite-media-player-loop).

### SiteInspire

- The rendered index is an image-led grid with category counts and filters for categories, styles, types, subjects, and platforms. Visible style filters include Typographic, Design & Art Direction, Minimal, Grid Layout, and Unusual Layout. Detail pages provide desktop/mobile captures, category labels, credits, and similar sites.
- Opened **Boc.Studio** (red opening, centered identity, large type, filterable work, repeated project imagery), **Union Boulangerie** (full-bleed video, hand-drawn display type, anchor navigation, location CTAs, method/sourcing/contact), and **The Surfer’s Journal Archives** (issues, collections, search, chronological cover index). Their gallery tags point to Big Type/Unusual Layout, Editorial/Minimal/Food & Drink, and Editorial/Minimal/Publishing.
- Useful: pair desktop/mobile captures with tags and credits; the related-sites rail supports adjacent discovery. Limit: gallery captures flatten interaction; collections and credit actions can require sign-in.
- Sources: [SiteInspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/), [Boc.Studio](https://boc.studio/), [Union Boulangerie](https://unionboulangerie.com/en/), [Surfer’s Journal Archives](https://archives.surfersjournal.com/).

### Awwwards

- The index links Site of the Day, nominees, winners, curated collections, Academy, directory, and blog. Detail records expose score breakdowns, technology tags, palette, highlighted elements, and a Visit action.
- Opened **EDOLUS** (dark cinematic WebGL/3D, pointer effects, scroll-driven scene), **The Romans** (animation, parallax, type-led agency storytelling), and **Tengile MalaMala** (photographic/editorial storytelling with persistent horizontal navigation and booking path). The Romans live site was blocked by a certificate error; the others opened. EDOLUS uses a full-screen experience with system readout and audio prompt, with little conventional text navigation. Tengile sequences hero, accommodation, story, conservation, and booking.
- Useful: element tags and color details make a technique searchable. Risk: awards over-represent spectacle; audio, WebGL, preloaders, parallax, and pointer-only behavior can cost performance, accessibility, and clarity.
- Sources: [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/), [EDOLUS](https://edolus.com/), [Tengile MalaMala](https://tengilemalamala.com/), [The Romans listing](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/the-romans).

### Land-book

- The index separates Websites, Sections, Mobile, New Motion, OG Images, and Headlines; filters combine industry, style, page type, typography, color, and platform. Detail pages use long desktop/mobile captures, palette chips, tags, author, related pages, and direct visit links.
- Opened **Weave** (3D mascot, maroon headline, pale canvas, metrics and product narrative; tagged animation/pastel/tech/SaaS), **Atalanta** (portfolio with background video, parallax, big type/footer), and **Ornn** (financial products for compute). The Weave page pairs the long capture with compact Save/Copy/Download/Visit actions.
- Useful: the Sections/Mobile/Motion split distinguishes reusable moments from full-page references. Do not assume an animation or parallax tag means the motion helps.
- Sources: [Land-book](https://land-book.com/), [Weave](https://land-book.com/websites/100373-weave-ai-to-measure-engineers-and-agents), [Atalanta](https://land-book.com/websites/99828-home-atalanta), [Driftime](https://land-book.com/websites/99820-home-driftime-R-the-guide-to-your-heroTM), [Ornn](https://land-book.com/websites/97398-ornn-financial-products-for-compute).

### Minimal Gallery

- The gallery has Websites, Templates, and Tools collections, with search, bookmarks, and a broad type taxonomy. The observed types include Product, Pricing, Research, Programming, Software, App, Editorial, and Photography.
- This makes it useful to find restrained work by purpose rather than searching “minimal” as a vague aesthetic. The page also exposes a Dark/Light control; that control belongs to the gallery, not necessarily to a listed design.
- Source: [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/).

### Refero

- The home URL returned no readable page body, but its public `/apps` route and indexed pages expose a library taxonomy for **page types** (including dashboards, product landing pages, developer pages, pricing, and about), **flows** (including onboarding, editing, booking, and upload/download), **UX patterns** (including search, sorting, checklists, and billing), and **UI elements** (including tables, tabs, navigation, sidebars, and animation).
- The site advertises actual product screens and patterns, which is a better source for a concrete interaction task than a gallery of isolated marketing covers. Treat captured screens as reference material rather than evidence of task success.
- Sources: [Refero](https://refero.design/), [Refero apps and pattern library](https://refero.design/apps).

### Mobbin

- The public landing page describes its library as real-world inspiration across more than 1,000 iOS and web apps and 200 sites, with new additions weekly. Pricing, login, and a free-join route are prominent.
- The public landing page was accessible; the deeper screen and flow library was not explored in this pass. Add a later pass to compare a few specific public onboarding, pricing, and settings examples, and record which content is gated.
- Source: [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/).

### Page Flows

- Public pages organize references as complete flows with screen recordings and step annotations, and advertise filtering by category, industry, and UX pattern. Public examples name areas such as onboarding, login, checkout, search, settings, and upgrade.
- The observed flow page requires sign-up to unlock detailed user flows. Treat it as a useful index for finding relevant tasks; mark its actual interaction evidence inaccessible until a flow can be viewed lawfully without an account.
- Sources: [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/), [public desktop guided-tour flow index](https://pageflows.com/screens/desktop/design-tools/view/tour/).

### Brutalist Websites

- The homepage is a dense, essentially unframed stream of site names, with few explanatory controls in the extracted first screen. Its introduction describes roughness and resistance to the comfortable conventions of contemporary web design; the index ranges across magazines, portfolios, art, music, commerce, and cultural sites.
- Useful search question: which conventions can be selectively removed while hierarchy and task completion stay intact? This is a territory reference, not a blanket usability recommendation.
- Source: [Brutalist Websites](https://brutalistwebsites.com/).

## Patterns to carry into future research

These are interpretations from the observations above, not claims made by the galleries:

1. Search by **design question**, not only by sector or overall style: type scale, image role, section transition, navigation shape, information density, motion, mobile behavior, or task flow.
2. Pair a visual gallery sample with a real product flow when the page has an action to perform. Refero and Page Flows offer task and component taxonomies; SiteInspire, Land-book, and Awwwards help explore art direction and composition.
3. Inspect a page beyond its hero: record section order, how it changes rhythm, where proof appears, image function/crop, CTA placement, footer, and mobile navigation. An attractive first viewport alone is not a complete UX reference.
4. For each reference, record **observed detail → user purpose → candidate principle → product-specific adaptation → risk to avoid**. Keep multiple candidates from different visual territories before choosing.
5. Preserve original purpose and authorship: references suggest principles. Do not copy their composition, illustration, wording, or proprietary product screens.
